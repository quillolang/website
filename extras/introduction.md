### Why .qwe?

Quillo files end in `.qwe` for ergonomics. Q, W and E sit side by side at the left end
of a QWERTY keyboard's top row, so the extension is one quick roll of the left hand's
little, ring and middle fingers, with no reaching and no looking down. You type it in
every `quillo run app.qwe`, every `use "lib.qwe"` and every new file name, so it should
be the easiest thing in the line to type.

It also reads as what Quillo is for: **Quit Worrying about Everything**. `quillo check`
finds mistakes before the program runs, text in pages is escaped, cross-site posts are
refused, sessions are signed, a payment sent twice is made once, and a transaction that
collides is tried again. What it does not take care of is listed in
[Possible or not](#possible-or-not), so you know what is still yours to worry about.
